# Per-Mode Self-Check

After writing the lyrics, run the checklist for the mode you used. If any item fails, revise before formatting the output.

## Imitation 仿写

- [ ] Is the emotional core still the source song's? (Not a different feeling wearing the same clothes.)
- [ ] Is the literal narrative replaced? (No source-song lines or signature imagery copied.)
- [ ] Does the new wording sound like the target artist — vocabulary, viewpoint, imagery, sentence patterns?
- [ ] Does the structure (section count, lines per section, chorus position) match the source?
- [ ] Is the emotional arc per section the same as the source?
- [ ] Does Verse 2 offer a different angle from Verse 1? (Not a diluted repeat.)
- [ ] Is there at least one "surprise" image per verse? (See anti-ai-patterns.md)
- [ ] Are per-line character counts within ±2 of the original? (±0 if melody-first is active.)

## Creation 创作

- [ ] Is the theme expressed clearly with concrete imagery, not abstract sentiment?
- [ ] Does the lyric sound like the target artist along the six dimensions (especially vocabulary and signature imagery)?
- [ ] Does the chorus have a memorable hook line — stands alone when read out of context?
- [ ] Are the rhymes natural, not forced (no awkward word choice just to land a rhyme)?
- [ ] Did you avoid lifting signature lines from the artist's catalog?
- [ ] Does Verse 2 shift angle from Verse 1? (Time, viewpoint, consequence, or deeper layer.)
- [ ] Is there at least one "surprise" image per verse?
- [ ] Do chorus peak lines end with open vowels? (Singability check.)

## Original 原创

- [ ] Did the style spec (abstract / mix / snippets) actually shape the wording, or is the lyric stylistically generic?
- [ ] Did you avoid "AI-flavored" patterns: over-balanced parallelism, vague abstract sentiment ("梦想 / 远方 / 心里的光"), filler adjectives?
- [ ] Does the imagery have specificity — at least a few concrete physical details?
- [ ] Does the structure match the theme (no through-composed for a hook-driven anthem, etc.)?
- [ ] If artist mix: is the result the *intersection* of styles, or an awkward stitch of two voices?
- [ ] Does Verse 2 shift angle from Verse 1?
- [ ] Is there at least one "surprise" image per verse?

## Melody-First 填词

- [ ] Does every line match the given character count exactly (±0)?
- [ ] If stress patterns were given: do stressed syllables land on strong beats?
- [ ] If pitch contours were given (Cantonese): do tone buckets align with melody direction?
- [ ] Are rhyme positions maintained despite the tight character constraints?
- [ ] Does meaning still flow naturally between lines? (No awkward enjambment forced by count constraints.)
- [ ] Are held-note positions filled with open vowels (not checked tones in Cantonese)?
