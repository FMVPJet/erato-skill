# Cantonese Register (粤语口语度)

Cantonese lyrics exist on a spectrum from fully literary (reads like Mandarin) to fully colloquial (reads like spoken Cantonese). The right position on this spectrum depends on the artist, era, and song mood.

## The spectrum

| Level | Description | Example | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 - 纯书面 | Standard written Chinese, could be Mandarin | 我不知道你是否明白 | 80s ballads, classical-style |
| 2 - 轻口语 | Mostly literary with a few Cantonese markers | 我不知道你是否明白呢 | 90s Cantopop mainstream |
| 3 - 混合 | Mix of literary and colloquial, natural flow | 我唔知你係咪明白 | Modern Cantopop default |
| 4 - 重口语 | Mostly colloquial, some literary for rhythm | 我唔知你明唔明㗎 | Conversational songs, 黄伟文 style |
| 5 - 纯口语 | Fully spoken Cantonese on paper | 我唔知你明唔明㗎喎 | Comedy songs, rap, very casual |

## Artist positioning

| Artist / Lyricist | Typical level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 林夕 (for 陈奕迅) | 2-3 | More literary, philosophical; uses 唔/係 but avoids heavy slang |
| 黄伟文 | 3-4 | More colloquial, urban, sharp; uses 嘅/咗/㗎 freely |
| 林若宁 | 2-3 | Similar to 林夕, slightly cooler |
| 周耀辉 | 2 | More literary/poetic, less colloquial |
| 陈奕迅 (overall) | 2-3 | Depends on lyricist; 林夕 songs more literary, 黄伟文 songs more colloquial |
| 谢安琪 | 3-4 | 黄伟文 writes most of her hits, so more colloquial |
| 容祖儿 | 2-3 | Mainstream pop, moderate |
| MC仁 / LMF | 5 | Full colloquial, rap |

## Common Cantonese markers by level

### Level 2 (轻口语)
- 呢 (sentence-final particle)
- 係 instead of 是
- 唔 instead of 不
- 嘅 instead of 的 (occasional)

### Level 3 (混合)
- All of level 2, plus:
- 咗 (perfective aspect, = 了)
- 嚟 (= 来)
- 喺 (= 在)
- 邊 (= 哪)
- 乜 (= 什么)
- 冇 (= 没有)

### Level 4 (重口语)
- All of level 3, plus:
- 㗎 / 嘞 / 喎 / 噃 (sentence-final particles)
- 嗰 (= 那)
- 啲 (= 些/点)
- 攞 (= 拿)
- 揀 (= 选)
- 嘢 (= 东西)
- 咁 (= 这么/那么)

### Level 5 (纯口语)
- All of level 4, plus:
- Multiple stacked particles (㗎喎、嘞喎)
- Cantonese-only idioms and slang
- Phonetic transcriptions of sounds (嗱、喂、哎)

## How to apply

1. **Determine the target level** from the artist positioning table above
2. **Stay consistent** within a song — don't jump between level 2 and level 5 in the same verse
3. **Chorus can be slightly more literary** than verses (it's more "sung" and less "spoken")
4. **Bridge can be slightly more colloquial** (it's often more intimate/confessional)
5. **When in doubt, default to level 3** — it's the modern Cantopop sweet spot

## Singability note

More colloquial Cantonese often has more checked tones (入声) because colloquial words tend to be shorter. Be aware that heavy colloquial at level 4-5 may create more tone-harmony challenges.
